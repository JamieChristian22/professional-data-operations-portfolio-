# New Microsoft Excel companion workbook

**EXCEL_Customer_Data_Entry_and_Quality_Control.xlsx** — editable Excel source-to-master customer data entry + quality assurance simulation.

- **START HERE**: operating guide and workbook navigation
- **QA Dashboard**: live intake KPI formulas and a disposition chart
- **Intake Review**: 12 simulated source records; 18 extra entry rows; formula-driven standardization, completeness, duplicate alerts, recommended disposition, reviewed outcomes
- **Approved Master**: nine accepted records with master checks
- **Exceptions Audit**: two duplicates and one missing-email hold with evidence and actions
- **Practice Source**: twenty independent simulated intake records for accuracy and typing practice
- **Practice Workbench**: 20 manual entry lines (blue cells), dropdown decisions, automated accuracy feedback
- **Answer Key**: expected 20 final entries and case-study dispositions
- **Lookups**: standards, statuses, and data governance controls

## Practice workflow
1. Open the practice source and workbook side by side.
2. Type the clean organization name, contact, normalized email, uppercase state, and decision in the blue practice workbench cells.
3. Inspect the G-J columns for completeness / duplicates / answer-key agreement.
4. Compare your work to the answer key, then review mistakes.
5. Try a new simulated intake record in row 19 or below of Intake Review.

**Note:** All people and companies are fictitious. Do not present these data or Excel scores as actual employer performance. Formula-detected email similarities are not permission to merge accounts without a human identity review.
