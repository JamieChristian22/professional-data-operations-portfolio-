## 📖 Project Overview

This project demonstrates a professional transcription, word processing, meeting documentation, and editorial quality-control workflow.

The scenario involves a fictional vendor-onboarding team that requires a simulated meeting recording and scripted dialogue to be transformed into organized, readable, and professionally formatted business documents.

The demonstration emphasizes accuracy, information preservation, clear communication, document consistency, and structured editorial review.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Convert simulated spoken dialogue into organized written documentation.
- Preserve essential facts, names, dates, figures, and decisions.
- Produce a clean-read transcript with speaker attribution.
- Prepare professional meeting minutes.
- Document assigned responsibilities and follow-up actions.
- Identify transcription and editorial errors.
- Apply consistent Microsoft Word formatting.
- Produce readable PDF documents.
- Maintain supporting quality-assurance evidence.

> **Portfolio disclosure:** This is a simulated transcription and document-production exercise using fictional meeting content and synthetic audio. It is not a recording of a real business meeting or evidence of paid transcription work.

```text
🎙️ Synthetic Audio + Scripted Dialogue
                  │
                  ▼
🎧 Source Material Review
                  │
                  ▼
⌨️ Transcript Preparation
                  │
                  ▼
👥 Speaker Attribution
                  │
                  ▼
🧹 Clean-Read Editing
                  │
                  ▼
📋 Meeting Minutes & Action Items
                  │
                  ▼
🔎 Editorial Quality Assurance
                  │
                  ▼
📄 Microsoft Word Formatting
                  │
                  ▼
📕 PDF Export & Review
                  │
                  ▼
✅ Document Delivery Package
```

### 1. 🎧 Source Material Review

Review the fictional meeting script and its synthetic audio companion to understand the discussion, terminology, and participants.

### 2. ⌨️ Transcript Preparation

Organize the dialogue into a structured transcript while preserving the substance of the source material.

### 3. 👥 Speaker Attribution

Associate each statement with the appropriate fictional meeting participant.

### 4. 🧹 Clean-Read Editing

Remove non-substantive verbal fillers and repeated starts where doing so does not alter meaning.

Preserve important dates, numbers, commitments, uncertainty, and decisions.

### 5. 📋 Meeting Minutes

Transform relevant discussion points into organized meeting documentation.

Separate confirmed decisions, unresolved questions, and assigned action items.

### 6. 🔎 Editorial Review

Check the transcript and minutes for formatting inconsistencies, unsupported changes, missing information, and incorrectly represented decisions.

### 7. 📄 Document Formatting

Use consistent headings, paragraph spacing, tables, and professional Microsoft Word layouts.

### 8. 📕 PDF Production

Prepare and review PDF output for document readability, formatting consistency, and content preservation.

## 🧪 Editorial Quality Assurance

### 🎯 Quality Control Objectives

The editorial review process focuses on:

- Accuracy of important facts
- Consistent speaker attribution
- Preservation of intended meaning
- Appropriate grammar and punctuation
- Correct dates and numeric information
- Accurate action ownership
- Identification of unresolved ambiguity
- Professional document presentation

### 📋 QA Control Matrix

| Control | Verification Objective |
|---|---|
| 👥 Speaker attribution | Statements are assigned to the correct speakers |
| 🔢 Numerical accuracy | Numbers and quantities match source material |
| 📅 Date verification | Dates and deadlines are represented consistently |
| 📝 Meaning preservation | Editing does not alter the substance of statements |
| 📋 Action item accuracy | Responsibilities and target dates are traceable |
| ❓ Ambiguity handling | Unconfirmed information remains identified |
| 📄 Formatting consistency | Headings, tables, and layout follow a consistent structure |
| 📕 PDF verification | Exported documents remain readable and consistent |

## 🔎 Editorial Defect Review

The project includes an 18-example editorial defect library for practicing and documenting quality-control judgments.

### Common Review Categories

| Category | Review Focus |
|---|---|
| ✍️ Grammar | Sentence structure and readability |
| 🔤 Spelling | Written accuracy |
| 🔣 Punctuation | Appropriate punctuation and clarity |
| 📅 Dates | Accurate representation of meeting dates and deadlines |
| 🔢 Numbers | Preservation of numerical information |
| 👥 Speaker labels | Consistent attribution |
| 📋 Decisions | Separation of confirmed and unresolved decisions |
| ⚠️ Ambiguity | Identification of information requiring clarification |
| 📄 Formatting | Consistent document presentation |

### 🛡️ Editorial Principles

1. Preserve factual information.
2. Do not invent missing meeting decisions.
3. Do not assign responsibilities without supporting source evidence.
4. Avoid changing the intended meaning during editing.
5. Document uncertainty rather than guessing.
6. Review final documents against the original reference material.

## 📝 Clean-Read Transcription Standards

### ✅ Appropriate Editorial Changes

- Remove non-substantive filler words.
- Correct obvious punctuation errors.
- Normalize presentation of clearly established dates.
- Improve paragraph structure.
- Use consistent speaker labels.
- Correct formatting without changing substance.

### ⚠️ Changes Requiring Caution

- Rewriting numerical information
- Changing speaker meaning
- Assigning an unidentified speaker
- Converting uncertain discussion into a confirmed decision
- Inventing missing words
- Adding unsupported responsibilities or deadlines

**Core principle:** Readability should never come at the expense of factual accuracy.
